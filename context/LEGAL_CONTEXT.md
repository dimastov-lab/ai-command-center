# Legal Projects — Context Pack

Updated: 2026-07-15
Status: Active

## Objective

Prepare accurate, internally consistent and evidence-based legal materials.

## Active Work Types

- claims and supporting documents;
- criminal-case evidence;
- correspondence analysis;
- payment calculations;
- procedural submissions;
- evidence preservation;
- document-package completeness reviews.

## Mandatory Separation of Work

Use separate stages for:

1. Evidence extraction
2. Chronology reconstruction
3. Financial calculation
4. Legal analysis
5. Document drafting
6. Independent consistency review

## Mandatory Rules

1. Do not invent facts, dates, payments or evidence.
2. Cite the source document for every material factual assertion.
3. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
4. Recalculate all monetary totals independently.
5. Preserve original wording where evidentiary significance matters.
6. Track missing documents explicitly.
7. Check party names, identifiers, dates and amounts consistently.
8. Do not silently change legal positions.
9. Flag limitation periods and procedural deadlines.
10. Treat legal conclusions as requiring professional verification where necessary.

## Default Case Structure

- parties;
- legal basis;
- chronology;
- amount claimed;
- payment history;
- evidence register;
- procedural history;
- risks;
- missing evidence;
- required next action.
